North star

Build useful, beautiful tools.

The long-term goal is to blur the line between functional systems and playful digital art. Every project—no matter how scrappy—moves that needle a little further.

  • Ship experiments that feel tactile and cinematic.
  • Document the messy bits so others can build faster.
  • Invest time where community impact is highest.
