My Goals

I like building things that are simple, fast, and actually useful—whether that's custom 3D-printed mouse shells, software tools, or documenting audio and PC gear.

Interests

Core Interests

Custom Keyboards

Fascinated by custom mechanical keyboards, layouts, switches, and tuning acoustic profiles for a perfect typing feel.

Hi-Fi Audio

Appreciation for in-ear monitors (IEMs), dynamic responses, and pure, reference-grade studio audio performance.

Custom Mouse Mods & 3D Printing

Tinkering with ultra-lightweight custom gaming mouse designs, modifying shape parameters for optimal grip ergonomics, and leveraging 3D printing to create beautiful, structural, physical hardware modifications.

Beyond the screen, I have a deep interest in custom mechanical keyboards, high-fidelity audio, and PC hardware. I'm always tinkering with my setup to get the best possible experience. This passion for hardware often inspires my digital projects, pushing me to create things that are both technically sound and beautifully designed.

What You'll Find Here

This site serves as a central hub for my work, thoughts, and discoveries. You'll find detailed write-ups of my projects, honest reviews of gear I use, and a collection of resources like spreadsheets and articles that I've found helpful.
