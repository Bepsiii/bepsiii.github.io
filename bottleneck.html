    The useful baseline

    One change. One repeatable test.

    01

    Repeat the same scene

    Use a built-in benchmark or a repeatable section of your game. Record the resolution, settings, and any frame cap.

    02

    Watch more than FPS

    Capture frame times, CPU and GPU activity, memory use, and storage activity while the issue happens.

    03

    Compare, then decide

    Change one setting and repeat. A spec list cannot tell you which upgrade will help your particular workload.
