Project notes

AscentCustoms was my project exploring 3D printed gaming mouse shells, shapes, and coatings. This page keeps the designs and development notes for reference.

Design approach

3D printing allowed rapid iterations on ergonomic features and customization: shape, size, weight, button placement, and material textures.
