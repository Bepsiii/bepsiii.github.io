Mission briefing
Why

Pushing mouse design beyond one-size-fits-all.

AscentCustoms was born from a need to tailor hardware to real players. With rapid 3D printing cycles, I can experiment with shapes, change button placement, and dial weight without waiting on a factory queue.

Every prototype is logged, photographed, and stress-tested by a tight community that isn't shy about feedback.

Toolkit
  • FilamentPETG-CF + PLA blends
  • Print volumeBambu X1 Carbon
  • FinishCoated, sanded, dyed
  • TestingGrip panels, glide packs, sensor alignments
Design pillars
Vision

Customization at hardware speed.

Traditional ODM mice rarely fit everyone. By iterating in CAD + slicer, I can roll out multiple shell sizes, vary wall thickness, and tune weight distribution for each grip profile.

  • Ergonomics driven by fingertip data.
  • Modular plates for switches and PCBs.
  • Finish treatments that feel premium, not prototyped.
Key features
  • Interchangeable backs for hand sizes
  • Tool-less latch system for quick PCB swaps
  • Target weight under 45g
  • Optional coated + hydrophobic finishes
