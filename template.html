About This Site Template

This is a template page. You can write all your custom HTML content for this specific page right here inside the layout container.

The navigation header and footer blocks are automatically injected from header.html and footer.html files via JavaScript during client loading.

All styles are controlled centrally in style.css, and the custom spotlights, interactive 2D glass float parallax transitions, and scroll triggers are initialized dynamically by main.js.
